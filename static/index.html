Questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. MedScan is a screening aid that helps you understand results and talk to a doctor. Scans in particular need a qualified specialist to read them.

What can I upload?

PDF, JPG or PNG from any lab or scan centre. Clear, well-lit photos of each page work best. Blurry or handwritten pages may be misread.

Where is my data kept?

In the database of the server running this site. If you host it for others, add login and encryption first.

What if it says emergency?

Go to the nearest emergency room or call your local emergency number. Do not wait for more analysis.
